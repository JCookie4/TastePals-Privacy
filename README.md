# TastePals Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

TastePals helps you and your friends decide where to eat and what to do. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. We built TastePals to be simple, and this policy follows suit.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and a unique account identifier from the sign-in provider. If you sign in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email address, in which case we receive Apple's private relay address instead. You may also create a nickname and choose an avatar and short bio within the app.

**Location.** With your permission, the app uses your device's location to find restaurants, bars, cafés, and activities near you. Your location is sent to Google's Places and Geocoding services to perform searches. We do not store a history of your locations. You can also type a location manually instead of using device location, and you can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

**Push notification token.** If you allow notifications, your device generates a push token that we store on your account so we can send you alerts — for example, when someone sends you a friend request, when a request is accepted, or when your group matches on a place. Tokens are delivered through Expo's push notification service. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings, and we will stop sending them.

**Content you create.** This includes places you save, reviews you write, star ratings, comments and likes on reviews, your friend connections and friend requests, and the swipe sessions you participate in (including which place you and your companions matched on).

**Information we do not collect.** TastePals does not show ads, does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not sell or share your personal information with data brokers. We do not collect contacts, photos, health data, or precise location history.

## How We Use Information

We use your information solely to operate the app: to sign you in, find places near you, run swipe sessions between you and your friends, display your saved places and match history, show your reviews to your connected friends ("TastePals"), and deliver the notifications you've opted into. We do not use your information for advertising or marketing.

## How Information Is Shared

**With other users.** Your name, nickname, and avatar are visible to users you connect with. Your reviews, review comments, and likes are intended to be visible to your connected friends. When you complete a session, each participant can see the match and the other participants' names.

**With service providers.** TastePals is built on Google Firebase (authentication, database, and server functions, operated by Google LLC), uses Google Maps Platform (place search and geocoding), Apple's Sign in with Apple, and Expo's push notification service for delivering notifications. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy commitments:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Expo Privacy Policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

**Legal requirements.** We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users.

We do not sell personal information.

## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Access is protected by authentication and server-side security rules so that, for example, only you can modify your profile and saved places, and only session participants can modify a session. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your account data and content remain stored while your account is active.

You can delete individual reviews within the app at any time.

**You can delete your entire account from within the app:** open TastePals, go to your Profile, tap the settings icon, and choose "Delete account." This permanently removes your profile, nickname, saved places, reviews, comments, likes, friend connections, and hosted sessions. This cannot be undone.

If you have trouble deleting your account in the app, contact us at the email below and we will delete your data within 30 days.

## Children

TastePals is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your Choices

You can revoke location permission in your device settings (search will require a typed location instead). You can turn off push notifications in your device settings. You can decline friend requests, and you control what reviews and content you post. You may delete your account at any time.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
